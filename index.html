About Our Institution

Building Knowledge. Shaping Character. Inspiring Futures.

Founded on the bedrock of holistic education, St. Andrews International Academy blends academic brilliance with ethical leadership.

Spread across a serene 25-acre lush green campus, our institution fosters an intellectually stimulating environment. We cultivate critical thinking, creative expression, scientific inquiry, and global citizenship, preparing our students to thrive in an ever-evolving world.

Message from the Principal
Dr. Radhika Sharma
Principal & Head of School

Welcome to St. Andrews International Academy. Over the past 25 years, our mission has been singular: to create a nurturing haven where intellectual vigor meets character and kindness.

In an era shaped by artificial intelligence and rapid technological acceleration, we believe the human core of education—critical inquiry, empathy, resilience, and collaborative problem-solving—remains paramount. Our academic modules are curated to foster deep conceptual understanding rather than rote memorization.

We welcome parents, scholars, and visitors to walk our corridors, engage with our faculty, and experience the warmth of the St. Andrews family.
